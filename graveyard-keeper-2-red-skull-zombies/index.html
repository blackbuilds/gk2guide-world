Graveyard Keeper 2 Red Skull Zombies: How to Build 5, 10 & 36 Red Skull Workers

Every red skull on a corpse becomes one perk slot on the zombie raised from it. Here are the step-by-step routes to a 5 red skull zombie (Bronze collar), a 10 red skull zombie (Golden collar) and 36+ red (Steel collar). Never raised a zombie? Run the basic pipeline on Zombies first, and do autopsy triage first so you don't cut up your best burial bodies.

TL;DR — the three builds

Target Collar (red cap) What you need Key move
5 red Bronze (0–5) Reanimation research, Bronze collar, 1 Reanimation Liquid, a 3–4 red corpse Swap one or two low-red parts for stored red parts until the panel reads exactly 5
10 red Golden (0–10) Reanimation II, Smithing 8 for the collar, better corpses from Brawns and Brains Build from the highest-red base, swap skin/bones/skull, top off with Solution for Zombies
36+ red Steel (0–99) Reanimation III, late-game corpses, Organ Improvers, embalming mixes Gold-grade red organs in every slot, red soft parts, then stack embalming

Always true: the corpse needs all six vital organs (skull, brain, heart, guts, bones, skin), its red count must fit the collar, and the device only runs on Envy (the green calendar icon) or while the Prayer for Storms blessing lasts.

Collar caps & exact counts

Collar Red skulls Jewelry Table recipe Buy instead Recipe unlocked by
Bronze Zombie Collar 0–5 1 Bronze Ingot + 1 Blue Crystal + 3 Faith Jeweler Reanimation
Golden Zombie Collar 0–10 1 Golden ingot + 1 Blue Crystal + 5 Faith (Smithing 8) Jeweler from trader level 2 Reanimation II
Steel Zombie Collar 0–99 1 Steel Ingot + 1 Blue Crystal + 10 Faith Jeweler from trader level 3 Reanimation III

What "exact" means in practice:

  • Over the cap is refused: an 11-red corpse cannot take a Golden collar ("This Zombie Collar isn't good enough for this corpse").
  • Under the cap works but wastes slots: a 7-red corpse on a Golden collar has 3 slots you paid for and can't use.
  • Later surgery must stay inside the cap, but a collar can be replaced by a higher one, so a 5-red worker can grow into a 10-red worker.

Blue Crystals: buy them from Gunter (they also turn up in the Swamp). Faith for collars comes from sermons — see Faith for zombie collars.

How the red count is built

A corpse's skulls sum its six body parts and 1–4 soft parts (Fat, Blood, Flesh) in its pockets. Bronze, Silver and Gold grades determine each part's skulls. Skin is a good example:

Skin grade Red variant Mixed variant
Bronze 1 red (or 0) —
Silver 2 red 1 red + 2 white
Gold 3 red 1 red + 3 white

So every part is a slot you can upgrade (a Gold red skin is three reds, a Bronze one is one or none), and you must read the panel on the body you hold — the same organ can be red, white or mixed. Store good red parts after every autopsy; they are what you spend on these builds. White skulls do nothing for a zombie; they matter only for graves.

Surgery without Surgeon's Errors

Each extraction runs ticks; Anatomy mastery controls success. At or above the Anatomy shown below, every tick succeeds. Fall short and you may lose the organ or get a Surgeon's Error: the organ is gone, 1 red and 1 white skull are removed, and an error in a vital slot stops resurrection.

Anatomy needed for a clean extraction, by organ grade:

Part Bronze Silver Gold
Skin 1 3 6
Bones 2 4 7
Skull 3 5 8
Heart 5 9 13
Brain 8 10 13
Guts 9 11 13

How to reach the number:

  • Eat a vegetable salad right before cutting: Bronze +1, Silver +2, Gold +3 Anatomy for a while. It is a Kitchen Table dish of carrots, beets and onions — see vegetable salad for Anatomy mastery.
  • Tools and perks: Anatomy tools such as the Surgeon's Kit add mastery; the Surgeon perk starts every extraction, insertion or swap with 2 successful ticks; Expert anatomist adds +4 at the Autopsy Table.
  • Plan cheap cuts: skin, bones and skull need far less Anatomy than heart, brain or guts, so build reds through those slots first.

Build 1: a 5 red skull zombie (Bronze collar)

  1. Unlock the basics: open the lab, research Reanimation, build a Resurrection Table (checklist on Zombies). The lab's starter chest holds your first Bronze collars and Reanimation Liquid.
  2. Get corpses that can reach 5. Early arrivals carry 0–1 red; after you ask Agatha, the Nun at the Crossroads, for better corpses in Brawns and Brains, they carry 1–5.
  3. Pick a 3–4 red base with all six vital organs. Keep red soft parts too.
  4. Find the weak slot — usually a Bronze skin or bone with 0–1 red.
  5. Eat a vegetable salad, then remove it.
  6. Insert a stored red part (a Silver 2-red bone or skin is typical). Stop at exactly 5; at 6 the Bronze collar refuses the body.
  7. Prepare with 1 Reanimation Liquid and the Bronze collar, then use the device on Envy.

Shortcut for zombies you already have: put a 3- or 4-red zombie back on the autopsy table, pull its weak bones, insert a red one, and you have a 5-of-5 worker without spending another Reanimation Liquid.

Organ Improver route: at the Formulation Bench, 1 organ + 1 red-skull Organ Improver makes the Gold version of that organ (Practical Potions; Anatomy 5 for skin, bones and skull, 7 for heart, brain and guts). The red-skull Improver is any alchemy mix whose runes add up to 4 red + 1 green — for example Copper Nugget + Red Mushroom, or Tooth + Melted Fat — and the Alchemist also sells it. The white-skull version (4 green, for example Flour + Flour) is for graves, not zombies. Full rune logic: organ improver & reanimation liquid recipes.

Build 2: a 10 red skull zombie (Golden collar)

  1. Open Reanimation II. Gunter's Necronomicon quest (it follows the Donkey's lead and asks for a book you print) opens the Reanimation Liquid technology; Reanimation II then gives the Golden collar and golden ingot recipes.
  2. Make the collar: 1 Golden ingot + 1 Blue Crystal + 5 Faith at the Jewelry Table, with Smithing mastery 8. Short? Eat a soldier's stew (+2 Smithing for a while) and craft straight away — or buy one from the Jeweler at trader level 2.
  3. Make Reanimation Liquid if you are out: Laboratory II (three slots), runes exactly 2 red + 1 green + 4 blue — e.g. Copper Nugget + Night flower + Night flower, or Clay + Alcohol + Salt. Gunter and the Alchemist also sell it.
  4. Pick the highest-red base, judged by the parts you will not touch (brain, guts and heart are expensive cuts).
  5. Swap the cheap slots — skin, bones, skull — for your best stored red parts. One working example: a 3-red skin, a 2-red bone and a 1-red heart dropped into a body that already carried the rest.
  6. Count to exactly 10. Over 10 the Golden collar cannot be selected; under 10 wastes slots. Top off with embalming if organs leave you at 7–9.
  7. Prepare with the Golden collar, then use the device on Envy.

Upgrade instead of starting over: swap a working 5-red zombie's Bronze collar for a Golden one, then add red parts until it reads 10.

Quest bonus: Albert's (the Plague Doctor's) Personal Zombie Zone quest asks for a zombie with at least 8 red skulls in a spot in his lab. It hands out 2 Golden Zombie Collars and 2 Reanimation Liquids, and that zombie then stops counting toward your zombie limit.

Build 3: 36 red and the Steel collar (the method)

The Steel collar caps at 99, so your corpses and materials are the limit. There is no single fixed body for 36; the method is:

  1. Unlock Reanimation III and craft the Steel collar (1 Steel Ingot + 1 Blue Crystal + 10 Faith), or buy it from the Jeweler at trader level 3.
  2. Start from late arrivals: after The House at the Crossroads, corpses carry 3–7 red and 20% have Gold organs. During a Prayer for Repose blessing, arrivals come from the next tier up.
  3. Turn every body part into a Gold red part with red Organ Improvers. Heart, brain and guts need Anatomy 13 to extract cleanly, so stack salad, tools and perks first.
  4. Keep red soft parts (Fat, Blood, Flesh can carry red too).
  5. Stack Solution for Zombies III (+3 red each) on top, then prepare with the Steel collar on Envy.

It is a late-game project, worth it only for the few mastery-gated stations where extra perk slots change output — haulers and porters work fine at 0–1 red (factory roles: Automation).

Embalming: add red skulls without surgery

Embalming changes skulls without cutting. Make mixes at the Formulation Bench and apply them at the Embalming Table; craft quality sets grade (I–III).

Mix Red White Made from
Solution for Zombies I / II / III +1 / +2 / +3 0 1 Alcohol + 1 Preservative
Injection for Zombies I / II / III +1 / +2 / +3 −1 / −2 / −3 1 Alcohol + 1 Preservative + 1 Comet's Tail
  • Preservative: water + flour is one simple mix. Alcohol: distil beer (cheap at the tavern) with a clay urn and fuel in the Distillation Cube.
  • "For zombies" = red; "for corpses" = white (those use Melted Fat instead of Alcohol and are for graves). The tooltips look almost the same, so read the name.
  • Solution beats Injection for zombies: whites do not matter, and the Injection also costs a Comet's Tail.
  • Embalming Table II works twice as fast as Table I.
  • Why it helps at 10: mid-game bodies often land at around 6 red; a +2 or +3 Solution closes the gap when no stored organ fits.

FAQ

Q: How many red skulls can each zombie collar hold?
A: Bronze 0–5, Golden 0–10, Steel 0–99. White skulls have no limit. If the corpse has more reds than the collar allows, the Resurrection Table refuses it.

Q: How do I make a 5 red skull zombie?
A: Take a 3–4 red corpse with all six vital organs, swap a low-red skin, bone or skull for a stored red part until it reads exactly 5, prepare it with a Bronze collar and 1 Reanimation Liquid, and resurrect on Envy.

Q: How do I make a 10 red skull zombie?
A: Research Reanimation II, craft a Golden collar (1 Golden ingot, 1 Blue Crystal, 5 Faith, Smithing 8 — soldier's stew gives +2), then build the corpse to exactly 10. At 11 the Golden collar cannot be selected.

Q: Can I add red skulls to a zombie after it is raised?
A: Yes. Put it back on the autopsy table and swap parts, as long as the total stays inside its collar. You can later replace the collar with a higher one.

Q: How do I get corpses with more red skulls?
A: Do Agatha's Brawns and Brains quest at the Crossroads: arrivals go from 0–1 red to 1–5, then 2–6 after the kit hand-over, and 3–7 after The House at the Crossroads.
