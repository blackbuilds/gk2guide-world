Step-by-step: Obtain / craft Dream Dust

Keep this numbered list contiguous (no mid-list ads).

  1. Free the Plague Doctor and open the Abandoned Tower using the basement route above. The tower’s starter chest commonly holds Water, Blood, flasks, and early Science materials — check it before foraging.
  2. Confirm the recipe path is available at Laboratory I after study (next steps). Alchemy in GK2 is rune-based: Dream Dust wants a green + blue combination, not a memorized GK1 ingredient list.
  3. Study Water and Blood at the Study Table (separately). Until studied, they often appear unusable / grayed out at the Laboratory even when you own them.
  4. Place Alchemy Flasks I on the Flask Shelf inside the lab. Flasks only in personal inventory do not count; each completed mix consumes a flask on the shelf.
  5. Brew at Laboratory I: select 1 Water and 1 Blood (order in the slots does not matter). Water supplies the green rune; Blood supplies the blue rune. Compatible researched green + blue substitutes can also work later — Water + Blood is the accessible starter pair most guides agree on.
  6. Confirm Dream Dust in inventory, then take it to the Plague Doctor for the Sweet Dreams / gate conversation.
  7. Follow Albert / continue the tower sequence when the quest advances — then leave this page for resurrection and workers.

FAQ

Q: What is Dream Dust used for in early game?
A: Clearing the Plague Doctor / zombie unlock gate (Sweet Dreams and related steps). Optimize/farming comes later — if at all on this page.

Q: Exact steps to get my first Dream Dust?
A: Free the Plague Doctor, open the Abandoned Tower, study Water and Blood, put Alchemy Flasks I on the Flask Shelf, combine 1 Water + 1 Blood at Laboratory I, then show the dust to the Plague Doctor.

Q: How do I unlock the Plague Doctor?
A: Progress the village/basement chain (meet Bally via Januarius, raise friendship, talk to Gerry, clear the cell rubble). Then craft Dream Dust and complete his tower conversation.

Q: What should I do immediately after?
A: Go to Zombies and resurrect/assign workers. Do not stay here grinding an alchemy database.

Q: Why do some guides disagree on steps?
A: Thin parallel articles + patch churn + rune substitutes. Prefer in-game Study Table / Laboratory results; we consolidate the agreed starter path and flag substitutes.

Q: Is Reanimation Liquid covered here?
A: No — that belongs with resurrection prep on the zombies hub and Wikily. This page stops at the Dream Dust gate.

Q: Do I need tech nodes before Dream Dust?
A: You need the tower lab stations unlocked via the Plague Doctor path. Broader early unlock order lives on Tech Tree Priority.
