Graveyard Keeper 2 Dream Dust Guide: Unlock the Plague Doctor Gate

In Graveyard Keeper 2, Dream Dust is the item that unlocks the Plague Doctor. It opens his alchemy lab and earns your first two zombie workers. This page walks you from the village quests to the finished brew. After that, the Zombies guide takes over.

TL;DR — Dream Dust in six steps

# Step Done when
1 Ask Januarius about fairies at the Village Tavern and meet Jully, the Head of the Village What the Fairy? completes
2 Reach village reputation 10 with Herm's wheat and the twins' pottery Good Fellow completes

Prerequisites: the quest chain

The chain looks like a detour, but every step is required.

  1. What the Fairy? Once the early burial-certificate quest is done, ask Januarius at the Village Tavern about fairies. He calls Jully, the Head of the Village, who asks you to prove you are "not an egoist." Before patch 1.007, some players saw placeholder text instead of Jully's name. That is fixed. The quest journal hints that Jully and Januarius may be the same person, but it does not state this outright.
  2. Supply and Demand. Talk to Herm, the village merchant. This opens the two small tasks that give you the reputation.
  3. Planted and Delivered. Buy wheat seeds from Herm (the quest asks for 16–20), grow them and hand over 5 Wheat. Reward: +5 village reputation, a Town Gratitude scroll and the Vegetable dishes technology.
  4. Two Peas in a Pod. Find the twins, Heffry and Jeffry, over the bridge in Twins' Land. Make peace between them, then bring 6 Clay Plate (build a Potter's wheel first). Reward: +5 village reputation, a Town Gratitude scroll and a Basic Rod.
  5. Good Fellow. With village reputation 10+, talk to Jully again. The journal then tells you that summoning a fairy needs Dream Dust, and that the Plague Doctor is in the dungeon.

Some guides describe step 5 as a friendship score of 10. The quest data lists it as village reputation 10. Either way, the two tasks above get you there. Town Gratitude scrolls also count towards sermon attendance later. See town gratitude & Faith basics.

Free the Plague Doctor (Dungeons and... that's it, probably.)

Step Action Cost or item
1 Return home and talk to Larry, the talking skull —
2 Open the closed hatch and go down into the dungeon under your house —
3 Clear the blockage 3 Bronze Detail + 3 Wooden Plank
4 Speak to the Plague Doctor behind the bars He gives you the secret combination
5 Open the prison bars into the Abandoned Tower Inquisitor's medallion (from the start of the game) + secret combination
6 Enter the tower and open the hatch to let him in —
7 Ask him how to make Dream Dust Quest ends

Rewards: +5 village reputation, +10 reputation with Albert, and the Alchemy, Basic technology. That technology unlocks the Flask shelf and the rest of the lab. One guide calls the skull "Gerry." In the game and on the quest wiki he is Larry.

The basement under your house is also where the Writing Desk area sits, so you'll be down there often anyway.

Packing list for the dungeon day

Carry everything in one trip so the chain doesn't stall halfway:

  • 3 Bronze Detail and 3 Wooden Plank for the blockage. One walkthrough ran short and had to go back up for another plank.
  • The Inquisitor's medallion. You pick it up at the very start of the game; check your bag or home chest.
  • A Science Kit or a few sheets of blank paper for the Study Table.
  • Food. Bread or berry pie, because dungeon work and studying both cost energy.
  • Free bag space for the tower's starter chest.

How to make Dream Dust

  1. Check the starter chest in the tower. It usually holds Water, Blood and flasks, so you may not need to gather anything.
  2. Read the recipe book at the lab. It shows Dream Dust as one green rune plus one blue rune.
  3. Get Science. Place a Science Kit on the Study Table for 30 Science. Blank paper also gives Science. To make more, see how to get Science for the Study Table.
  4. Study one Water and one Blood. Each study costs 1 Science. Studying keeps the item, reveals its runes for good, and pays tech points.
  5. Load the Flask shelf. Each Alchemy Flasks I you place becomes 5 flasks for the lab. Flasks in your backpack don't count. You can make more at the furnace from glass.
  6. Brew at Laboratory I: 1 Water (green) + 1 Blood (blue). Slot order doesn't matter.
  7. Take the Dream Dust to Albert in the Abandoned Tower. He only checks it, so it stays in your bag for the fairy summoning later.
Ingredient Runes Notes
Water 1 green From the Well; study first
Blood 1 blue From corpses on the morgue table; study first
Peat 1 green + 1 blue Works alone; the Compost Pile makes it from 8 Leaf
Ash 1 green + 1 blue Works alone
Leaf 1 green Swap-in for Water
Blueberry 1 blue Swap-in for Blood

Why guides list different ingredients: Dream Dust has several working combinations (Water + Blood, Leaf + Blood, Peat alone). They're all correct. The lab only checks that the runes add up to exactly 1 green and 1 blue, and there are 23 working mixes. Once you know how runes work, any studied pair with those runes will do.

Keep a spare Dream Dust or two: showing it to Albert does not consume it, and Dream Dust is needed later for the fairy summoning. It is also an ingredient in Crop Boosting Fertilizer I and Fancy Incense at the Formulation Bench. Later, the town Alchemist sells it.

FAQ

Q: How do I make Dream Dust in Graveyard Keeper 2?
A: In the Abandoned Tower, study Water and Blood at the Study Table, put Alchemy Flasks I on the Flask shelf, then combine 1 Water and 1 Blood at Laboratory I. Any ingredients whose runes add up to exactly 1 green and 1 blue work.

Q: How do I unlock the Plague Doctor?
A: Ask Januarius about fairies at the Village Tavern to meet Jully, the Head of the Village. Reach village reputation 10 for Good Fellow, then go home and talk to Larry the skull, open the hatch and clear the blockage (3 Bronze Detail, 3 Wooden Plank).

Q: How do I get village reputation 10?
A: Do Herm's two early tasks. Bring 5 Wheat for Planted and Delivered, and 6 Clay Plate to the twins for Two Peas in a Pod. Each gives +5 village reputation and a Town Gratitude scroll.

Q: Can I make Dream Dust from one ingredient?
A: Yes. A single Peat or Ash already carries 1 green and 1 blue rune, so one of them alone brews Dream Dust once it has been studied.

Q: What do I get for bringing Dream Dust to the Plague Doctor?
A: Sweet Dreams pays +10 reputation with Albert (the Plague Doctor), a bronze Story, the Alchemist's Clothes and 2 free zombie workers, then asks you to follow him through the hatch in the Alchemy Lab.

Q: Why is my Water or Blood greyed out at the Laboratory?
A: The Laboratory refuses any ingredient whose runes you have not studied. Study one of each at the Study Table first; a Science Kit placed on the table gives 30 Science to spend.
