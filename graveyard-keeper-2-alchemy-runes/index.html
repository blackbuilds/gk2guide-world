Graveyard Keeper 2 Alchemy & Runes: How-to (Not a Formula DB)

Checked for Graveyard Keeper 2 · patch 1.005 — numbers may change; re-check after updates.

This Graveyard Keeper 2 page teaches the alchemy workflow: when to study, which stations matter, and how rune thinking replaces memorized GK1 ingredient lists. It deliberately is not a formula encyclopedia — Wikily owns that database.

Primary early payoff: craft Dream Dust and clear the Plague Doctor gate → Dream Dust → Zombies.

TL;DR — Rune workflow in five checks

# Check If failing
1 Alchemy lab accessible (Abandoned Tower after Plague Doctor path) Finish basement / tower unlock on Dream Dust
2 Ingredient studied at the Study Table Research before brew — grayed lab slots usually mean “not studied”
3 Correct rune colors for the target product Think green/blue/red contributions, not GK1 recipes
4 Alchemy Flask on the Flask Shelf (not only in inventory) Place flasks; each mix consumes shelf stock
5 Correct Laboratory tier for ingredient count Lab I for two-ingredient starter crafts; higher tiers when recipes need more slots

Outbound for formulas: Wikily — Alchemy

How GK2 alchemy differs from “recipe lists”

Habit from other games / GK1 GK2 approach
Memorize exact ingredient pairs forever Study items to reveal rune properties, then combine matching colors
Dump raw ingredients into a cauldron Study Table first, then Laboratory with flasks loaded
Copy a blog’s full reagent table into muscle memory Use one starter craft (Dream Dust) to learn the loop; look up later formulas on Wikily
Skip Science management Studying spends Science; paper / Science Kits feed the Study Table → Tech Priority

Dream Dust is the tutorial product: 1 Water (green) + 1 Blood (blue) after both are studied, with Alchemy Flask I on the shelf, at Laboratory I. Other researched green+blue pairs can substitute later — Water+Blood is the accessible starter most guides agree on (as of 2026-09-26).

Stations checklist

Station Job Done when
Study Table Research items → reveal runes + grant Tech Points Target reagents no longer grayed for alchemy
Flask Shelf Holds Alchemy Flasks the Laboratory consumes Flasks physically on the shelf
Laboratory I Combines two researched reagents by rune Starter crafts (Dream Dust) complete
Higher Laboratory tiers More ingredient slots for advanced mixes (e.g. some Reanimation Liquid paths) Unlock when a recipe demands it — confirm in-game
Distillation Cube (adjacent) Produces intermediates (e.g. Melted Fat) used by resurrection / other loops Built when Reanimation path needs it

Energy note: study + brew sessions fail more often when you are exhausted — stabilize food first → Energy/Bread.

Order Question If No
1 Do I need this item for a named gate? Don’t tourist the alchemy tree
2 Studied? Research each reagent
3 Rune colors match the target? Pick different studied reagents or study new ones
4 Flask shelf stocked? Place flasks
5 Craft succeeded? Read grayed reasons; don’t invent substitutes from GK1

FAQ

Q: Is this every alchemy recipe?
A: No. This is the workflow. Formula DB → Wikily Alchemy.

Q: How do runes work at a high level?
A: Studied ingredients contribute color properties (commonly discussed as red / green / blue). The Laboratory combines those properties into products. Exact charts belong on Wikily.

Q: Why won’t Dream Dust craft?
A: Almost always missing Study Table research or flasks on the shelf. Full gate checklist → Dream Dust.

Q: Do I need alchemy before zombies?
A: You need Dream Dust / Plague Doctor progress for the early zombie gate. Ongoing resurrection also uses alchemy intermediates (liquids, distillation). Pipeline → Zombies.

Q: Should I study everything I own?
A: Study what unlocks a named gate or reveals runes you need. Kits are precious — couple with Tech Priority.

Q: Can I use Graveyard Keeper 1 alchemy tips?
A: Not safely. GK2 is study + rune driven. Re-verify every habit in GK2.
