Graveyard Keeper 2 Zombie Combat: Squads, Gear, Defense Power and Every Battle

Clearing the Town in Graveyard Keeper 2 means battles: timed waves of enemy zombies against your squads, barricades and the Keeper himself. Most of the work happens before the fight — gear, defense power and the right day. This page covers all of it, with the numbers for all 17 battles.

TL;DR — Zombie combat in six lines

  • A squad is 4 zombies, each with armor plus a pike or a bow. No swords.
  • Defense power = barricades and towers + weapon and armor power of armed squad zombies + mercenaries.
  • Most battles start on Sloth; five start on any day.
  • Win by taking every enemy flag and holding them until the waves end.
  • Battle Pike or Battle Bow + Iron Armor gives 16 power per squad — the mid-game standard.
  • Winning zombies earn red tech points; mercenaries don't.

How battles work

Herbert gives you a red banner before the first battle, and the Barracks open for squads afterwards. From then on:

  1. Pick the day. Most battles start only on Sloth — the fast travel guide has the weekly route.
  2. Meet the requirements. Each battle sets a minimum defense power and a squad limit, and hired mercenaries count as one squad.
  3. Fight the waves. Enemies come down one to three lines. You win by capturing every enemy flag and still holding them all when the waves run out.
  4. Don't lose the base. The battle ends at once if enemies take your base or the Keeper's health hits 0.

Four battles have no flags — Market Square Purge, Operation POTATO: Part II, the Final Battle and the Sewerena. There you only hold the base until the waves end.

Moving troops. One creator's walkthrough moves squads by carrying the banner forward and notes that flags only fall if you push onto them. Treat this as a player tip.

Why a battle won't start: it isn't the right day; the Keeper's tool belt lacks a sword or body armor; he carries more than one item on his head; or defense power is short.

Defense power: the number that gates every battle

Source Defense power
Squad zombie with armor and a pike or bow Weapon power + armor power
Barricade or archer tower (not counted in Sewer Brawls) +2 / +4 / +6 by tier
Mercenaries 8, then 16 and 24 after upgrades

A zombie missing either piece adds nothing and doesn't fight. Per zombie, gear gives:

Loadout Per zombie Per squad of 4
Light Pike or Reinforced Bow + Bronze Armor 2 8
Battle Pike or Battle Bow + Iron Armor 4 16
Heavy Bow or Knight's Spear + Steel Armor 6 24
Heavy Bow + Master Armor (if your zombies can wear it — see below) 7 28

Squad slots come from Defender's Emblems, up to 4. You earn one each from the Port and Bathhouse Basement break-throughs, Active Scouting in the Eastern Districts, and Central Districts Purge: Part I. Patch 1.006 made emblems indestructible, because losing one could block a place.

Zombie weapons and armor

Zombie fighters have 100 health. A hit deals the attacker's damage minus the target's armor; enemy zombies wear none.

Bows pause 1 s between shots; pikes 0.65 s. The Simple Pike (+1) can't be obtained in 1.007.

The Tin Can zombie perk takes 1 more off every hit. Rusty Armor comes from the prologue and adds no power, so it only gets a zombie into a fight.

Master Armor on zombies: it needs Masterwork Equipment and Smithing 15; patch 1.007 fixed Tempering Expert unlocking Master Armor. Wikily lists it for zombie fighters, while one creator's squad video says masterwork gear isn't available to zombies. Check your Barracks before you craft it for them.

Barricades, towers and mercenaries

Barricades and archer towers are built in the Barracks' Military Building Area and placed from the Command post during a battle.

Defense Power Health Extra
Barricade I / II / III +2 / +4 / +6 400 / 600 / 800 —
Spiked Barricade I / II / III +2 / +4 / +6 250 / 450 / 600 Hits back 3 / 5 / 7
Fortified Barricade I / II / III +2 / +4 / +6 600 / 800 / 1000 —

War Supplier and War Engineer improve barricades and towers.

Mercenaries are four fighters, two pikes and two bows, who take one squad slot and add 8 power. The Merc Leader upgrades them to 16 (3 Iron Ingot) and 24 (3 Steel Ingot). One payment lasts until you win a battle with them:

Levelling your fighters

Fighter perks sit on the Smithing tree: Impaler and Blindseye (200 points), Tin Can and Undead Resilience (400). Red points from wins pay for them — see combat perks & mastery. Fighters don't need top bodies; the red skull guide treats soldiers as a separate goal from factory workers.

FAQ

Q: What is defense power in Graveyard Keeper 2?
A: The number next to the sword before a battle. It adds up your barricades and archer towers, the weapon and armor power of every armed squad zombie, and the mercenaries (8, 16 or 24). A battle won't start below its requirement.

Q: Which battles can I start on any day?
A: Break Through to the Port, Sewer Brawl I and II, Break Through to the Bathhouse Basement and the Sewerena. Every other battle waits for Sloth, except in the window after Herbert hands you the red banner.

Q: How do I get more squad slots?
A: With Defender's Emblems, up to 4 slots. You earn one each from the Port and Bathhouse Basement break-throughs, Active Scouting in the Eastern Districts, and Central Districts Purge: Part I.

Q: Do Damage Potions help my zombie squads?
A: No. Damage Potions only raise the Keeper's hits, plus the mercenaries' once the Merc Leader has upgraded their gear. For zombies, drink a Zombie Motivator Potion, which halves the pause between their attacks.

Q: Are mercenaries worth hiring?
A: Early, yes: 5 silver adds a full squad worth 8 defense power. The price climbs after each win with them up to 1 gold, and they take one of your squad slots and earn no tech points.

Q: Why won't my battle start?
A: Check the day, that the tool belt holds a sword and body armor, no more than one item carried on the head, and defense power at or above the requirement.
