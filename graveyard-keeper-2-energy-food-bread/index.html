Graveyard Keeper 2 Energy & Insanity: Early Food Loop (Bread First)

Checked for Graveyard Keeper 2 · patch 1.005 — numbers may change; re-check after updates.

This Graveyard Keeper 2 page fixes Day-1 friction: exhausted, insane, out of food. The hero asset is the early food ladder — not a cooking encyclopedia.

TL;DR — Early survival loop

  1. Stop grinding before meters force a bad burial or failed craft.
  2. Sleep / rest on a schedule — not “one more task.”
  3. Repair the kitchen (Broken Oven + Broken Kitchen Table), keep Fuel stocked, then build wheat → dough → bread.
  4. Use bread as the practical staple; add soup/pie later as next rungs only.
  5. Run an insanity reduction checklist whenever the meter climbs (insanity shrinks usable energy headroom).
  6. Return to systems play → Beginner · Zombies.

Ignore: gourmet endgame food meta; full alchemy-as-food deep dive; cloning all cooking recipes.

Energy vs insanity (what each meter does)

Meter Job (plain English) Practical habit
Energy Limits how much physical work you can do before you must rest/eat Top up with cooked food in the field; sleep when the day is shot
Insanity Rising stress pressure; high insanity reduces how much energy you can effectively use Rest, eat calming foods when you have them, stop stacking failed horror loops
Interaction Empty energy + high insanity compounds Day-1 mistakes (bad triage, failed crafts) Fix meters before Dream Dust / autopsy marathons

Do not invent a numeric “danger line.” Read the HUD and food tooltips on your build.

Repair the kitchen first

Public kitchen how-tos (AllThings.How, as of 2026-09-26) agree on this starter gate:

Station Common repair materials Job after repair
Broken Oven Pieces of Stone Hot meals / baking
Broken Kitchen Table Wooden Planks Prep / cold dishes / slicing

Confirm exact counts on the repair prompt — they are easy to misremember across patches. Stock Fuel for the oven (Firewood → Fuel via a Firewood Shed is the usual early path). The kitchen table prep steps do not consume Fuel; oven recipes do.

FAQ

Q: Why am I always out of energy in Day 1?
A: You’re probably over-grinding without a kitchen/bread/sleep loop. Repair the oven and table, stock Fuel, build wheat→dough→bread, and set a stop rule before empty.

Q: How do I make bread as early as possible?
A: Repair kitchen stations, grow wheat, make dough with water, bake with Fuel. Unlock Millstone/Bread techs if the recipe is still gated. Repeatability matters more than a single lucky craft.

Q: How do I reduce insanity?
A: Rest, eat foods whose tooltips help, and stop stacking stressful failures. Confirm exact debuffs on your HUD.

Q: What food is “good enough” before full cooking unlocks?
A: Bread first; soup/pie as later rungs only. We will not list every recipe — see Wikily/Games.gg outbound for DB seekers.

Q: When should I stop and sleep vs push one more task?
A: Stop when another failure would wreck a corpse, craft, or sermon. Meters recover; bad burials don’t.

Q: Is this a cooking guide?
A: No — early survival ladder only. Full cooking encyclopedia is an explicit non-goal.
