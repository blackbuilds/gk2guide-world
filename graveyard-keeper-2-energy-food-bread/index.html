Graveyard Keeper 2 Energy & Insanity: Early Food Loop (Bread First)

In Graveyard Keeper 2, energy pays for almost everything you do, and insanity quietly shrinks the energy bar. This page explains how the two bars work and when sleep actually helps. It also covers the kitchen loop (bread first, then berry pie) and which foods and drinks to keep on your hotbar.

TL;DR — The early survival loop

Step What to do Why
1 Eat the 8 Bread from the home chest before you sleep on day one Free energy while the kitchen is still broken
2 Repair the Broken Oven and Broken Kitchen Table Cheap: 2 Piece of stone and 2 Wooden Plank
3 Build a Firewood shed and keep Firewood in it The oven burns Fuel on every recipe
4 Wheat → Flour → Dough → Bread, then Berry pie Pie adds 30 energy and removes 3 insanity
5 Sleep only when you see the tired icon Sleeping while rested does not lower insanity
6 Keep Beer for bad days -12 insanity per drink

How energy and insanity work

Crafting, gathering, digging and studying cost energy. In a fight, attacks use stamina instead. Insanity is the second bar, and it takes space from the first:

  • Maximum energy = 100 − insanity. At 30 insanity your bar holds 70 energy, however much you eat.
  • Insanity stops at 80, so the worst case is a 20-energy bar.
  • With Iron Will, insanity tops out at 70, so your energy bar cannot fall below 30.

That makes insanity a hidden energy tax. Keeping it low is usually cheaper than eating more.

What raises insanity

  • Grave work: digging, burying and exhuming.
  • Autopsies on the morgue table.
  • Making headstones and fences, and placing them on or removing them from a grave.
  • Crafting zombie collars, Urns with Ashes, Tooth, Leather Scroll and the Improve-organ recipes.
  • Collecting honey from a Beehive.
  • Some food and drink: Soldier's ale (+20), Baked meat (+10), Flesh (+10), Burger (+5), Sandwich (+5), Lasagna (+1) and Swamp mushroom (+1).
  • Lack of sleep: while it lasts, every point of energy you spend adds half a point of insanity.

Morgue and graveyard days are the expensive ones. Plan your reducers around them.

Sleep: when it helps and when it doesn't

  • Lack of sleep starts after two full days without sleep. You'll see a tired icon near your bars.
  • Sleeping until energy is full ends it and removes 20 insanity. This is the only way sleep lowers insanity.
  • While Lack of sleep lasts, every reducer works at half strength. A beer removes 6 instead of 12.
  • Going to bed when you are not tired refills energy but removes no insanity. Several early-game guides stress this point, and it matches the rule above.
  • The game saves automatically after sleep. That is the one good reason to sleep early.

A practical rhythm: work until the tired icon shows up, finish one last low-risk job, then sleep to full. Stack your morgue and graveyard work before the tired icon appears, not after, because tired digging doubles as an insanity source.

Wrath, the sermon day, is the one day you can't afford to arrive empty. See plan food and sleep around sermon day for the weekly Faith schedule.

Repair the kitchen first

Both kitchen stations in your home start broken, and both are cheap to fix:

Station Repair cost Job
Broken Oven → Kitchen Oven 2 Piece of stone Bakes bread, pies, fish and mushrooms; burns Fuel; finishes on its own once started
Broken Kitchen Table → Kitchen Table 2 Wooden Plank Dough, salads and snacks; you work it by hand
Millstone Unlocked in the tech tree 4 Wheat → 5 Flour (8 with the Miller perk)
Well In the yard Water for dough
Firewood shed Tech tree (Bronze Melting technology) 2 Firewood → 20 Fuel; chop logs at the Chopping Spot

Fuel is the usual blocker. Every oven recipe burns it: bread and baked mushrooms take 10, baked fish takes 15, and berry pie takes 20. Keep firewood in the shed and the oven draws fuel on its own.

Early food ladder: wheat → flour → dough → bread

Stage Station Recipe (per craft) Tech
Wheat Garden beds Seeds from Herm, the village merchant Gardening
Flour Millstone 4 Wheat → 5 Flour Millstone
Dough Kitchen Table 6 Flour + 2 Water → 6 Dough Bread
Bread Kitchen Oven 4 Dough + 10 Fuel → 4 Bread (6 with Cook) Bread
Berry pie Kitchen Oven 4 Dough + 6 Berry + 20 Fuel → 4 Berry pie (6 with Cook) First dishes

Bread gives 18 energy and is also an ingredient for sandwiches, burgers and Breadcrumb Bait. Berry pie is the upgrade: 30 energy and −3 insanity per pie. Berry bushes around your base regrow, and dough is the only real bottleneck. Many players queue pies constantly and stop worrying about energy altogether.

Take finished food out. The oven holds a finished set until you empty it, and the next set won't start until you do. If the oven looks idle with fuel in it, it is usually full.

Perks that pay off early: Cook raises oven output (4 → 6 bread or pies). Heat Saver cuts oven fuel (bread 10 → 5). Miller raises flour from 5 to 8. Researcher shortens Study Table sessions.

Mushrooms and fish are free early energy. Brown mushroom patches near your base respawn every few days. One beginner walkthrough says to bake them rather than eat them raw. Fishing spots refill slowly, so fish each spot until it runs dry. One walkthrough quotes baked fish at 30 energy, but the item data lists 40. Trust the tooltip on your save.

Several late dishes restore more than their tooltip says. Energy never goes above your current maximum, so eat them when the bar is low.

Food buffs: eat before the job

Some dishes add temporary mastery. Eat one right before the matching task:

Dish Energy Buff
Vegetable salad (bronze / silver / gold) 15 / 20 / 25 +1 / +2 / +3 Anatomy
Soldier's stew 25 +2 Smithing
Bowl of sauerkraut (bronze / silver / gold) 20 / 25 / 30 +1 / +2 / +3 Book writing
Muffin 20 +2 Farming
Pumpkin soup 20 +2 Building
Toast with onions 20 Beer thirst: beer removes 24 insanity instead of 12

The Anatomy salad is the one that matters for body work. For when to eat it during extraction, see food buffs for surgery. Soldier's stew adds +2 Smithing for a while, which helps you reach the Smithing 8 the Golden zombie collar needs.

Simple rule: pies for daily upkeep, beer after a morgue day, wine or chocolate if insanity has crept past 40. Remember that everything counts half while you have Lack of sleep.

Cooking with zombies

A zombie can work the kitchen stations. Before patch 1.006, kitchen zombies could burn ingredients without producing the dish. That is fixed, so update the game before you hand the oven to a worker. If a zombie seems to eat inputs, first check fuel and whether the output slot is full.

FAQ

Q: What is the maximum energy in Graveyard Keeper 2?
A: 100 minus your current insanity. Insanity stops at 80, so the worst case is a 20-energy bar. With Iron Will, insanity tops out at 70, so your energy bar cannot fall below 30.

Q: Does sleeping reduce insanity?
A: Only when you have Lack of sleep, which starts after two full days awake. Sleeping until energy is full then removes 20 insanity. Going to bed when you are not tired refills energy and saves the game, but it does not lower insanity.

Q: What is the best early food?
A: Berry pie: 4 Dough, 6 Berry and 20 Fuel in the Kitchen Oven make 4 pies, each worth 30 energy and -3 insanity. Bread (18 energy) comes first, and baked fish (40) and baked mushrooms (27) are strong free extras.

Q: How do I make bread?
A: Repair the Broken Oven (2 Piece of stone) and the Broken Kitchen Table (2 Wooden Plank). Grind 4 Wheat into 5 Flour at the Millstone, mix 6 Flour and 2 Water into 6 Dough at the Kitchen Table, then bake 4 Dough with 10 Fuel into 4 Bread.

Q: What lowers insanity fastest?
A: Wine (-25 to -35 by quality), Chocolate (-20), sleeping off Lack of sleep (-20) and Beer (-12, or -24 after Toast with onions). Berry pie (-3) and writing at the Writing Desk (-1 per tick) handle the small daily amounts.

Q: Why does my oven stop cooking?
A: Finished food sits in the oven until you take it out, and the next four do not start until you do. Also check the fuel: the oven burns Fuel from a Firewood shed, and 2 Firewood make 20 Fuel.
