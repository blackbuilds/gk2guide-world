About GK2 Guide

GK2 Guide publishes independent how-to guides for Graveyard Keeper 2: Day-1 priorities, corpse triage, zombie workers, tech order, and mid-game systems. We focus on decision trees and checklists — not a full wiki, item encyclopedia, or quest database.

This site is not affiliated with Lazy Bear Games, tinyBuild, or Steam. Screenshots and key art remain © their respective owners.

Patch freshness

Public pages carry a stamp such as:

Checked for Graveyard Keeper 2 · patch 1.005 — numbers may change; re-check after updates.

We update the patch number on the stamp when we re-check guides after game updates. It does not refresh automatically every day. After a patch, confirm costs and UI labels in your own save — the footer disclaimer is the same rule.
