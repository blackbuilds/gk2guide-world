How tech points & mastery work (plain English)

Concept What it means
Tech Points (red / green / blue) Spendable unlock currency. Red leans labor/materials; green leans nature; blue leans spiritual / study-heavy knowledge
Study Table Place an item, spend Science (and sometimes Faith) → gain Tech Points and reveal alchemy runes; item usually stays in inventory
Science Decompose Clean Paper / Science Kits at the Study Table; kits are far more efficient than single sheets
Mastery Five Keeper tracks (Building, Smithing, Farming, Book writing, Anatomy). Recipes list a required level; low mastery makes ticks probabilistic
Inspiration / perks Separate talent trees fed by Inspirations; many claim levels cost Faith after the first free tier
Faith coupling Weekly sermons fund Inspiration claims and some studies — treat sermon week and spend week as one loop → Faith

Efficient early habit: Unlock Must-tier gates → earn through play → avoid shopping the whole tree.

Soft-lock bridge (mid-game quest → mine / porters → later conveyors):

  • Red / locked tech nodes often wait on quest progress, not just points — don't tourist-unlock past a red gate you cannot open yet.
  • Typical mid-game arc: finish the NPC quest that opens the mine / automation segment → place a miner + porter (trash zombie is fine for hauling) → unlock supplier / factory modules only when workers exist → conveyors later — confirm node names in-game.
  • Weekly check: "Which NPC quest unlocks the node I want?" If the branch is red-locked, advance that quest before spending elsewhere.
  • Deep logistics / conveyors → Automation. For reanimation timing, trust the quest UI + Wikily over Demo-era caps.

Early unlock decision tree (Week 1)

  1. Are you blocked on zombies? → Prioritize Dream Dust path + Reanimation prerequisites → Dream Dust / Zombies.
  2. Else, are you blocked on Faith spends / church? → Prioritize sermon/church quality nodes + run the weekly loop → Faith.
  3. Else, is energy collapsing your play sessions? → Bread / farming QoL → Energy/Bread.
  4. Else, do you already have workers idling in chaos? → Zombie Assistant / logistics only now → Automation.
  5. Else → Stay on Must-tier production/Science nodes; do not tourist the late tree.

Worked examples (general approach — check node costs in-game):

  1. Blocked on Science → Unlock Writing Supplies / Study Table feed (Clean Paper, Ink, Science Kit I — confirm labels in-game) before cosmetic Theology furniture / lighting "because church looks nice." Science engine first; Theology decor after the Faith loop needs it.
  2. Red quest-lock on the branch you want → Do not buy shiny side nodes past it. Open the task / quest menu, find the NPC that gates that branch, finish that step, then spend.
  3. Faith week vs Inspiration ocean → Preach on sermon day; keep enough Faith for early map movement / weekly prep; claim Inspiration only for a named Keeper skill bottleneck (e.g. a craft you cannot make yet).
  4. Quest-driven unlocks (Demo-era direction only) → Prefer "what does the active quest need?" over spending points randomly. FAQ

Q: What should I unlock first in week 1?
A: Writing Supplies (Science loop), basic wood/stone/Tool Rack, sermon tools, then the Reanimation path when the story reveals it. See the ranked TL;DR table and confirm costs in-game.

Q: How do I earn tech points / mastery efficiently early?
A: Study Table for green/blue-heavy gains; gathering/crafting for red. Raise Mastery via Inspiration talent nodes and temporary tool/food buffs. Couple with Faith.

Q: Which unlocks gate zombies, church upgrades, or factory?
A: Zombies → Dream Dust + Reanimation on Zombies. Church → Faith. Factory → Automation. Confirm prereq nodes in-game.

Q: What can wait until mid-game?
A: Supplier/factory depth beyond Zombie Assistant, niche alchemy, cosmetic/late branches — Later/Skip tiers above.

Q: How does Faith interact with tech progress?
A: Faith claims Inspiration levels and some studies; sermons are the steady source. Practical loop on the Faith page.

Q: Should I trust older “best technologies” guides?
A: Only after matching current patch labels/costs. Prefer in-game tree + this dated priority opinion over GK1 Reddit threads.

Q: Where do exact node RGB costs and labels come from?
A: Prefer Wikily / graveyardkeeper2.cc early-tech / in-game tooltips. Costs quoted in YouTube tip videos can go out of date — treat them as hints and confirm in-game. Re-check after every patch.

Q: Why is a tech node red / locked even though I have the points?
A: Mid-game branches often soft-lock behind NPC quests (mine / porters / later conveyors). Ask weekly which quest unlocks the node you want, then advance that story beat before tourist-spending elsewhere → Automation.
