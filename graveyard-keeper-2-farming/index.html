Graveyard Keeper 2 Farming: Garden Beds, Fertilizers and Zombie Gardeners

Farming in Graveyard Keeper 2 feeds your kitchen, your Likes and several Town upgrades. The rules are less obvious than they look: after planting, your own mastery stops mattering and the bed takes over. This page covers crops, bed Farming, fertilizers and the zombie gardener that runs it all for you.

TL;DR — Farming in six lines

  • 9 crops: seven grow on Garden Beds; Hops and Grapes need Vine Trellises.
  • You plant, the bed grows. Your Farming covers 4 planting ticks; the bed's Farming covers the other 8.
  • Bed Farming is 2 at first, 4 and 8 with Garden Upgrades I and II.
  • Fertilizers stay on the bed until replaced. Peat (+1) is the first one.
  • Zombie gardener: Gardener Stand + Tablets + at least a Bronze Shovel.
  • Grade: cells 11 and 12 pay one grade above the seed.

How a harvest works

A bed runs through 12 cells.

  1. Planting (cells 1–4). You plant 4 seeds over 4 ticks (1 energy each), using your own Farming. These cells pay nothing, but a failed one is lost.
  2. Growth (cells 5–12). The bed fills the rest by itself, one tick every 2:00, using the bed's Farming.
  3. Harvest. You pick the ripe bed by hand. Each successful cell pays one item: odd cells a seed, even cells the crop. Every harvest also gives 2 Leaf and some green tech points.

Success chance. At or above a crop's requirement, every tick succeeds. Below it, the chance is your Farming divided by the requirement — 4 against 5 is 80%. A strong bed can fill up to 3 cells per growth tick.

So a perfect bed pays at most 4 seeds and 4 crops, plus perk bonuses.

Grades. For Cabbage, Onion, Pumpkin and Grapes, cells 1–10 pay the seed's grade and cells 11–12 pay one grade higher. Gold seeds always pay gold.

Bed Farming and garden upgrades

The bed has its own Farming, and your mastery doesn't change it. One creator's fertilizer video makes the same point: a level-9 Farming Keeper still had a bed at 4.

Upgrade Bed Farming Growth tick Cost Technology
Start Garden Beds 2 2:00 — Gardening
Garden Upgrade I 4 1:45 10 Peat, 18 Bronze Nails, 9 Wooden Plank Garden Improvement I
Garden Upgrade II 8 1:30 9 Farming Fertilizer II, 8 Iron Detail, 4 Oiled Beam Garden Improvement II
Vineyard Upgrade Vine Trellises 6 → 11 — 6 Farming Fertilizer III, 6 Advanced Detail, 4 Oiled Beam Vineyard Improvement

At bed Farming 2, only Wheat grows reliably; Carrot is about a coin flip per tick. Garden Upgrade I makes Carrot safe and Flax likely. Garden Upgrade II covers everything up to bronze Cabbage.

The Harvest Prayer blessing (God of nature) cuts 0:20 off every growth tick while it lasts; the prayer unlocks with Fertilizers IV. See Faith & sermons.

Fertilizers

A bed has no fertilizer slot until Fertilizers I (1 slot); Fertilizers II adds a second. A fertilizer stays through every harvest until you replace it.

All except Peat are mixed at the Formulation Bench. Crop Boosting: the in-game tooltip lists +1 cell of growth, and one creator's fertilizer video reads the same +1 from the tooltip; Wikily's farming page gives +2. This guide plans on +1, the tooltip figure.

Which to use. If the bed is below the crop's requirement, add Farming (Peat, then Farming Fertilizer). Once every tick succeeds, switch to Headstart or Crop Boosting to reach cells 11–12 and the higher grade. The Dream Dust in Crop Boosting is the same item as in the Dream Dust walkthrough.

Zombie gardener setup

  1. Get zombies first (the Plague Doctor's Sweet Dreams gives your first two).
  2. Research Zombie Gardening. This gives you the Gardener Stand, the Lifting Crane and one Tablet per Garden crop.
  3. Build a Gardener Stand in the Garden: 4 Wooden Plank, 2 Bronze Detail, 6 Bronze Nails.
  4. Put a zombie on it and give the gardener a shovel. Both farming videos use at least a Bronze Shovel.
  5. Place a Tablet on each bed. The gardener replants empty tabled beds with that crop and harvests ripe ones.
  6. Stock seeds in the zombie's inventory or the garden storage.
  7. Add a Lifting Crane (8 Wooden beam, 3 Bronze Gear, 4 Iron Detail) to carry crops into Garden Storage. Storage II has 30 slots; Storage III has 50.

Zombie Vineyard does the same for the Vineyard, with its own stand, a Porter Station and Hops and Grapes tablets. Zombie perks Dirt Digger (+2) and Crop Planter (+4) raise a gardener's planting; Fertilizer Expert gives +3 on fertilizer crafts.

One creator keeps a single stack of each seed and sells the rest.

Beehives

After Beekeeping, a Beehive goes in the Vineyard (cost: 2 Wooden Plank, 1 Bronze Detail, 6 Bronze Nails). Emptying it takes Farming 3 and gives 3–6 Honey and 1–2 Beeswax; it refills over 8 cells of 1:00. Wild hives give 1–2 Honey.

What to do with the harvest

  • Eat it. Wheat → flour → bread is the early energy line — cook what you grow.
  • Sell for Likes. Crops carry thumbs-up at Herm, the Vegetable Stand and the Brewery; spread them across traders because each has its own weekly cap. More in make money.
  • Upgrade shops. Vegetable Stand II takes 40 Cabbage and Stand III 40 Onion — see restoration kits.
  • Flax → Flax Thread for the Tailor and for bows: a Battle Bow takes 6 — see zombie squads.

FAQ

Q: What Farming do I need to plant each crop?
A: Wheat 2, Carrot 4, Flax 5, Beet 6, Hops 7. Cabbage needs 8, 10 or 12 for bronze, silver or gold seeds, Onion 10 to 14, and Pumpkin and Grapes 12 to 16. Below the number a planting tick can still succeed, just less often.

Q: Why doesn't my Farming mastery speed up the crops?
A: Your mastery only covers the 4 planting ticks. The other 8 cells grow on the bed's own Farming (Garden Beds 2, 4 or 8 with upgrades, Vine Trellises 6 or 11) plus its fertilizers.

Q: How long does a fertilizer stay on a bed?
A: Until you put another fertilizer in its slot. It keeps working through every harvest, and at most two work at once once Fertilizers II gives beds a second slot.

Q: How do I set up a zombie gardener?
A: Research Zombie Gardening, build a Gardener Stand in the Garden, put a zombie on it with at least a Bronze Shovel, and place a crop Tablet on each bed. Keep seeds in the zombie's inventory or the garden storage.

Q: How do I grow silver or gold crops?
A: Plant a better seed, or push more cells to success. The last two cells pay one grade above the seed, so Headstart and Crop Boosting fertilizers move a harvest up a grade.

Q: Where does Peat come from?
A: A Compost Pile makes 2 Peat from 8 Leaf; Compost Pile II makes 5 from 16. Every harvest gives 2 Leaf, and the Compost Chemist perk doubles the Peat.
