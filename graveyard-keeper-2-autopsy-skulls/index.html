White skull vs red skull (GK2 purpose split)

Treat this as a purpose split, then confirm the readout on the corpse you are holding:

Skull type Primary job Practical rule
White skulls Graveyard / burial quality A white skull row counts toward grave quality once matched by decoration / wreath quality on that grave
Red skulls Zombie worker perk slots Each red skull unlocks a zombie perk slot after resurrection — and for burial, each red row consumes decoration capacity without giving a positive quality point
Both matter Same corpse can lean yard or labor Decide the goal before you cut

Done when: You can say out loud whether today’s corpse is for yard or worker before you operate.

Public GK2 references (Wikily + Fextralife Corpse Quality, as of 2026-09-26) agree on this purpose split. Per-organ ±skull values vary by organ version — read the body in front of you; do not memorize a universal “always remove X” list.

Decision tree: What do I do with this corpse?

  1. Is the Resurrection Table / zombie pipeline online?
    - No → Prefer bury “good enough” bodies; cremate/dispose truly bad ones; don’t stockpile forever without a plan.
    - Yes → Continue.
  2. Is your goal right now graveyard quality or labor?
    - Yard quality → Keep / improve white-facing outcomes; remove red-facing organs when a clean extraction helps covering → Bury.
    - Labor → Prefer higher red-skull bodies with vital organs intact → Zombies hub.
  3. Is this corpse actively bad for the yard if buried as-is?
    - Yes, and not a worker candidate → Cremate (or river dispose if Crematorium is not online yet) rather than poisoning plots.
    - Yes, but salvageable with autopsy → Autopsy workflow below, then re-check.
    - No → Bury or resurrect per goal in step 2.
  4. Are you tempted to use GK1 organ memory?
    - Stop. Re-check the GK2 corpse panel or open Wikily GK2 corpses — never trust GK1 Fandom by default.

Autopsy workflow (steps)

Step What you do Done when

Extraction outcomes (conceptual): Operations run on ticks tied to Anatomy mastery. Enough successes keep the organ as an item; mid results remove it ruined; too few successes can leave a Surgeon’s Error (worse skull balance). Exact tick tables → Wikily Reference.

Early vs later: Early — practice reading skulls and avoiding obviously bad burials. Later — deliberate worker prep once collars and perk slots matter. Organ-by-organ encyclopedia → Wikily, not here.

Preparing bodies for zombies

Once resurrection is available:

  1. Pick corpses for labor, not every body.
  2. Prefer more red / fewer white for workers; keep vital organs intact (missing core body parts block resurrection — soft parts like Blood/Flesh/Fat are a different category).
  3. Match collar tier to the red-skull count you are aiming for (bronze collars cover a limited early range — confirm on the collar tooltip).
  4. Hand off to the zombies pipeline for Resurrection Table, assignment, collars. → Graveyard Keeper 2 Zombies
  5. Mid-game automation still cares about worker quality. → Automation · Red Skull Zombies

Do not expand this section into a full max-red recipe guide.

Wreaths / covers / decorations rule

Agreed conceptual rule across Wikily and Fextralife Corpse Quality (as of 2026-09-26):

  • Each red skull row must be covered by decoration / wreath capacity before white rows start contributing positive graveyard quality points.
  • A red-covered row still does not score a positive quality point — it only spends covering capacity.
  • Once all useful white rows are covered, extra decoration on that corpse cannot invent more white skulls.

Exact decoration point values per fence/headstone upgrade: confirm in-game and on Wikily; we omit a fake covering spreadsheet here.

FAQ

Q: When should I bury vs resurrect vs cremate/dispose?
A: Bury for yard goals when white rows can be covered; resurrect when you need workers and the body is a red-skull candidate; cremate/dispose when the body hurts the yard and isn’t worth prep. Use the decision tree above.

Q: What are white skulls for vs red skulls for in Graveyard Keeper 2?
A: White leans graveyard/burial quality (after decoration covers red rows). Red leans zombie perk slots and consumes covering capacity on graves. Do not treat GK1 Fandom numbers as GK2 facts.

Q: What makes a “bad corpse” for the graveyard?
A: A body whose red rows eat decoration capacity so white rows never score — or a body you bury without enough headstone/fence quality. When in doubt, autopsy to diagnose, then bury / cremate / hold for zombies.

Q: Which autopsy actions are safe early vs save for later?
A: Early: learn to read skulls and avoid obviously harmful burials. Later: deliberate worker prep and embalming once resurrection and collars matter. Don’t delay first workers for perfect reds.

Q: How does this page connect to zombie workers?
A: This page decides which bodies become workers; Zombies covers unlock, Resurrection Table, assignment, and optimize stages. Deep red-skull optimization → Red Skull Zombies.

Q: Why do other guides disagree / why might GK1 advice be wrong?
A: Patch churn + franchise bleed. Always prefer the in-game GK2 corpse readout; when public GK2 guides conflict on organ math, we link Wikily instead of inventing a compromise table.

Q: Do embalming solutions belong here?
A: Conceptually: corpse vs zombie solutions shift white/red without surgery. Exact mix recipes → Wikily embalming table.
