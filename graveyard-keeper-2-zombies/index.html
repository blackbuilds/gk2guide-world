Stage 2 — Resurrection Lab, table & first workers

High-level steps (confirm labels on your build):

Step Action Done when
1 Open the Morgue door into the Resurrection Lab (common costs cited: 1 Bronze Detail + 1 Melted Fat) Lab room accessible; starter chest often has Reanimation Liquid + Bronze Zombie Collars
2 Research Reanimation on the Anatomy & Alchemy tree (revealed after repairing / progressing the resurrection device; needs Alchemy/Distillation and related prereqs) Resurrection Table I blueprint available
3 Build Resurrection Table I in the Morgue (common build: 2 Oiled Planks, 4 Polished Stone, 3 Glass) Table interactable
4 Prepare a complete corpse (vital organs present) with 1 Reanimation Liquid + 1 Zombie Collar on the table Prepare / strap succeeds
5 Activate the Resurrection Device when the lab is powered — commonly on Envy day (storm / green calendar mark), or via Prayer for Storms when that effect is active Zombie spawned
6 Confirm first free/early workers from quest rewards vs crafted labor You know starter labor vs ongoing resurrection

Vital organs: Keep the core body-part set intact. Soft pocket parts (Blood / Flesh / Fat) are a different category — missing a core part commonly blocks prepare/resurrect.

Error messages: Do not trust invented toast text from other sites. Read the on-screen reason (missing part, zombie limit, power spent, collar mismatch). Steam Update 1.005 fixed several Resurrection Table / “cannot resurrect despite requirements met” cases — update before debugging forever.

Stage 3 — Put zombies to work

Topic Practical rule
Equipment Open the zombie inspection menu and give the required tool for the job; tools raise the matching Work Mastery track
Assignment Carry the zombie to a workstation that accepts zombie labor; place it and queue a recipe the zombie can perform
Mastery If a craft is locked, pending crafts often show when skill level is too low — train or pick a simpler recipe
Pathing Keep beds / stations / storage reasonably close; blocked routes idle workers
Inputs / outputs Empty input chests or full output storage stop the loop — fix logistics before hiring more bodies
Babysitting Watch one full work cycle before mass-hiring

FAQ

Q: How do I unlock zombies at all?
A: Clear the early Dream Dust / Plague Doctor gate (often grants early free workers), then open the Resurrection Lab and research Reanimation for ongoing resurrection. Detailed craft/unlock steps live on Dream Dust.

Q: What do I need before the Resurrection Table is useful?
A: Lab access, Reanimation researched, a complete corpse, Reanimation Liquid, a suitable Zombie Collar, and a powered resurrection window (commonly Envy day).

Q: How do I assign a zombie to a job?
A: Equip the required tool, carry the zombie to a compatible workstation, place it, and queue a recipe it can perform. Confirm tools and a clear path, then watch one cycle.

Q: Why won’t my zombie work / what are common errors?
A: Usually missing assignment, tools, inputs/outputs, mastery, or pathing. Read the on-screen reason — see the troubleshooting table. Do not trust invented message text without matching your UI.

Q: When should I care about red skulls vs just any worker?
A: After basic labor exists and a station is the bottleneck. Each red skull opens a perk slot conceptually; precise thresholds belong on Red Skull Zombies. Prep bodies on Autopsy.

Q: What’s next after basic workers?
A: Supplier/porter/factory automation checklist → Automation, with tech gates on Tech Priority.

Q: Is this the full red-skull crafting guide?
A: No — intro only. Deep optimization is the red-skull spoke.
