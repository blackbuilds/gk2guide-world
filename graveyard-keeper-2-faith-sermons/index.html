Graveyard Keeper 2 Faith & Sermons: How to Get Faith (Weekly Loop Checklist)

Faith in Graveyard Keeper 2 comes almost entirely from one weekly sermon. This page covers how to unlock the church, what decides how much Faith a sermon pays, where town gratitude comes from, and what to spend Faith on first.

TL;DR — Weekly Faith loop

  1. Unlock the church through Jack's boat quest → the Twins → the Nun → Holy Moly!.
  2. During the week, earn Likes (the thumbs-up meter many players call town gratitude): thumbs-up trades, burial certificates, town orders, gratitude scrolls.
  3. Keep church quality at least as high as your Likes — attendance is the lower of the two.
  4. On Wrath, hold one sermon with a prayer your attendance can pass (the window shows the success chance).
  5. Spend the Faith the same week — milestones first, then collars, research and Inspirations.

How a sermon pays Faith

Step Rule
Parishioners The lower of your Likes and your church quality. No Likes, or zero church quality, and nobody comes
Likes are spent Every sermon uses up Likes, so you refill them each week
Minimum Each prayer has a minimum attendance; below it the sermon cannot start
Success chance Parishioners ÷ the prayer's difficulty, capped at 100%
Base reward Faith and bronze per parishioner — paid even if the sermon fails
Success bonus Extra Faith per parishioner, bronze per point of graveyard quality, and the prayer's blessing if it has one

Worked example: Basic Prayer I with 20 parishioners and graveyard quality 30 is a certain success. Faith = 20 × 0.25 = 5, plus 20 × 0.3 = 6 on success, 11 Faith in total. Money = 100 bronze base plus 300 bronze bonus, 4 silver.

Unlock the church (if Faith is still "impossible")

There is no early hidden Faith source: you need the church, and it is unlocked through a story chain. Start it early — it is easy to leave as a side quest and lose weeks of Faith.

Step Action
1 South of the village, Jack argues with two brothers. Later he is found at the Twins' Land and starts Jack in a Boat: bring him 5 Wooden Planks, then a Bronze Axe and Bronze Hammer (hand over exactly what his request names)
2 Persuade the Twins to row Jack — this needs 10 Village reputation; their small quests (such as clay plates, Pottery I) help you get there
3 Jack sails. A few days later his letter arrives, and a Nun comes to the Village Tavern — talk to her
4 Her Holy Moly! quest opens the church. Clean it, add a few benches, and hold the first sermon, which can happen on any day
5 From then on, one sermon each Wrath

Tech gates that often block this chain: Woodworking I, Bronze Works, Pottery I — see Tech Tree Priority.

Where Likes (town gratitude) come from

Source How it works Tip
Thumbs-up goods Some items in a trader's list show a thumbs-up and a quantity. Selling them pays Likes plus coin Each trader shows its weekly Likes cap above the trade window. Wheat and carrots are easy early picks
Burial certificates Every burial earns a certificate; selling them at the tavern pays Likes Higher-tier certificates from better corpses sell for more
Town Gratitude scrolls Quest rewards; each one read gives +5 Likes. Januarius also sells them, but they are expensive Store them in the church; don't read one when you are within 5 of the cap
Town orders Accepted only on Pride at the order board; a finished order pays Likes when collected Repeatable orders can be taken again next Pride
Zombie gardener crops Zombie gardening tech + Gardener Stand + a bronze shovel + crop tablets on each row Crops keep coming without your energy; sell the thumbs-up ones weekly

How many do you need? Aim for at least 10 Likes before you preach. That clears the minimum for every early prayer and makes Basic Prayer I certain. More Likes means more parishioners, more Faith and more money.

Raising the cap: your Likes bar has a maximum. Restoring town houses gives Green Scrolls; Linda uses them to attract new people (on Lust), which raises Town quality and the maximum Likes. Help with the town earlier and the cap grows.

Church quality: only as high as your Likes

Church quality is the most parishioners the church can hold. It never adds anyone past your Likes, so decorating beyond your Likes is for looks only.

Lever Effect
Clean up and add benches The first reliable jump after Holy Moly!
Theology fittings (lighting, carpets, later items) Unlocked as tech points allow
Choir Holds 5 zombies: 1 church quality plus 1 per zombie
Organ Holds 1 zombie: 5 church quality plus 1 for its zombie
Musician zombie perks Raise each choir/organ zombie to 2 (Novice Musician), 4 (Skilled) or 7 (Maestro)

Spare low-red zombies are ideal choir singers — see Zombies.

Basic Prayer vs Faith Prayer (and the early options)

The Nun gives you Basic Prayer. Better prayers are written at the Writing Desk; a written prayer is not used up by preaching.

Prayer Minimum Certain at Faith per parishioner Bronze per parishioner Blessing
Basic Prayer I 4 8 0.25 5 —
Faith Prayer I 7 15 0.3 5 —
Money Prayer I 7 15 0.2 10 —
Prayer for Storms 20 40 0.4 20 Resurrection works on any day for 1 h 30 min
Hypno Prayer I 60 120 0.3 10 Clears Excessive zombies when granted

How to choose:

  • Under 15 parishioners → Basic Prayer I. A failed roll still pays base Faith but loses the success bonus, so a certain Basic sermon usually beats a risky Faith Prayer.
  • 15+ parishioners → Faith Prayer I for Faith, Money Prayer I when coin is the bottleneck.
  • Check the success chance on the sermon window before you start; it is shown there.

Preacher perks: Priest raises Faith per parishioner (Basic Prayer I goes from 0.25 to 0.35); Cardinal raises bronze per parishioner; Performer raises the success bonus.

Sermon money: on success, every point of graveyard quality adds 5–30 bronze depending on the prayer. Clean burials pay twice — see Autopsy & Skulls. For the other income routes that sit around the weekly sermon, see how to make money fast.

Plan energy for the sermon day too — plan food and sleep around sermon day.

What to spend Faith on (early to mid game)

Spend Cost Why
Fast-travel milestones 7 of the 11 cost Faith (1–8 each, 23 in total); 4 are free, including the Inquisition Base The biggest time saver in the game
Zombie collars Bronze 3, Golden 5, Steel 10 Faith each Every zombie you raise needs one
Study Table research Some items cost Faith as well as Science Unlocks runes and recipes · how to get tech points
Inspiration claims The first level is usually free; later levels cost Faith Inspiration points for your mastery trees

Milestone order: activate the Village (2 Faith) first — you go there constantly for shops and certificates — then the Port Area (3). Crossroads and Graveyard cost 1 each, the Vineyard 2, the Quarry 6 and the Swamp 8. Early Faith spent on movement pays back every day.

Keep a few Faith in reserve for early quest requests, then invest the rest.

Holy Routine (sermon Inspiration)

Holy Routine counts every sermon you hold, of any prayer type:

Level Sermons in total Faith to claim
I 1 Free
II 3 1
III 7 5
IV 15 8
V 30 10
VI 100 20

Progress is a running total, so claiming a level never resets it. Another reason never to skip a Wrath.

FAQ

Q: How do I get Faith in Graveyard Keeper 2?
A: Hold a sermon at the church once a week on Wrath. Faith is paid per parishioner, and parishioners are the lower of your Likes (town gratitude) and your church quality, so raise both before you preach.

Q: How do I unlock the church and sermons?
A: Finish Jack's boat quest (Jack in a Boat), persuade the Twins to row him out (this needs 10 Village reputation), then meet the Nun at the Village Tavern after Jack's letter. Her Holy Moly! quest ends with your first sermon, which can be held on any day; after that sermons run every Wrath.

Q: How do I get town gratitude (Likes)?
A: Sell the goods marked with a thumbs-up to traders (each trader shows a weekly cap), sell burial certificates at the tavern, complete town orders, and read Town Gratitude scrolls from quests (+5 each). A zombie gardener can grow the crops you sell.

Q: Basic Prayer or Faith Prayer?
A: Basic Prayer I needs 4 parishioners and is certain at 8. Faith Prayer I pays a little more Faith per parishioner but needs 7 and is only certain at 15. With fewer than 15 parishioners, Basic Prayer I is the safe pick.

Q: What should I spend Faith on first?
A: Fast-travel milestones near your routes (Village 2 Faith, Port Area 3), then zombie collars (3, 5 or 10 Faith), Faith-costing Study Table research and Inspiration claims.

Q: What is Holy Routine?
A: An Inspiration that counts your sermons: 6 levels at 1, 3, 7, 15, 30 and 100 sermons. The first claim is free; the rest cost 1, 5, 8, 10 and 20 Faith.
