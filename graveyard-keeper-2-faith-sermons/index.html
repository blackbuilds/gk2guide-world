Graveyard Keeper 2 Faith & Sermons: Weekly Loop Checklist

Checked for Graveyard Keeper 2 · patch 1.005 (as of 2026-09-26) — numbers may change; re-check after updates.

This Graveyard Keeper 2 page is a weekly Faith loop (prep → sermon → spend), not a church wiki. Use it when progression feels soft-locked on currency.

TL;DR — Weekly Faith loop

  1. Unlock the Temple via the early Jack / Twins / Nun boat chain if sermons are still unavailable.
  2. Improve Temple Splendor / church quality and keep the town goodwill meter (thumbs-up; some guides say Likes / Gratitude) topped up.
  3. Prep sermon (prayer choice + quality + goodwill).
  4. Deliver Mass once per week on Wrath day (dark-red calendar mark — confirm icon on your build).
  5. Spend Faith on Inspiration claims and Must-tier progression — don’t infinite-hoard. → Tech Priority
  6. Repeat weekly until the soft-lock clears.

Unlock the Temple (if Faith is still “impossible”)

Public Faith how-tos agree: there is no early hidden Faith workstation — you need the Temple.

Step Action
1 Progress Jack’s boat requests (commonly Wooden Planks + Bronze Details — confirm quest counts)
2 Help the Twins (commonly Clay Plates via Pottery I) so the boat can leave
3 After Jack’s letter, meet the Nun at the Village Tavern
4 Temple unlocks; first sermon often ties to the Holy Moly! quest line

Tech gates that commonly block this chain: Woodworking I, Bronze Works, Pottery I — see Tech Priority.

Goodwill vs quality — practical tradeoffs

Guides variously say Likes, Gratitude, or show a thumbs-up meter. Whatever your UI calls it:

Situation Lean toward Why
First sermons while learning UI Stable Temple quality baseline Avoid compounding bad yard + bad prep
Soft-locked on a spend Maximize Faith outcome for this sermon Clear the bottleneck, then normalize
Yard recently wrecked by bad burials Fix corpses/decor first Prep can’t fully paper over garbage burials → Autopsy
Extra goodwill materials scarce Don’t bankrupt next week’s loop Weekly reliability > one spike
Goodwill already full Don’t waste single-use goodwill pages Overflow is wasted

Refill goodwill via burial certificate sales, thumbs-up shop goods, town orders, and quest rewards — then preach.

FAQ

Q: How do I generate Faith reliably each week?
A: Unlock the Temple, top up Splendor + goodwill, preach on Wrath day, then spend. Reliability comes from routine, not a one-time quest dump.

Q: What should I prepare before a sermon?
A: Use the prep checklist — Temple state, goodwill budget, prayer, Wrath-day timing, spend plan, energy.

Q: How does church/graveyard quality affect outcomes?
A: Attendance is limited by the lower of Temple quality vs goodwill; bad burials and empty goodwill both shrink the flock. Exact Faith formulas: confirm on the Prayer Report — we omit fake spreadsheets.

Q: What should I spend Faith on first?
A: Inspiration claims that raise Mastery/perks you need, plus Must-tier progression — see Tech Tree Priority.

Q: Which calendar days change the loop?
A: Wrath day is the weekly Mass window most guides agree on. Other special-day names stay omitted until you confirm them on your calendar.

Q: Is Holy Moly required reading here?
A: It’s blocker context for the first sermon. For quest steps, outbound Wikily — we don’t clone the quest tree.
