Graveyard Keeper 2 Zombie Mastery & Perks: Why Your Zombie Won't Work, and How to Level It Up

Your new zombie is standing at the bench doing nothing. That is not a bug: every zombie in Graveyard Keeper 2 starts with 0 mastery. This page explains how zombie mastery adds up, which tools to hand out, how perk points are earned, and how to spend a small red-skull budget on the perks that matter.

No zombie yet? First resurrect your first zombie with the table, collar and Reanimation Liquid.

TL;DR — Get an idle zombie working

How zombie mastery adds up

A zombie has the Keeper's five masteries: Building, Smithing, Farming, Book writing and Anatomy. For any recipe, its mastery is:

nodes it bought on that tree + the tool in its hand + a perk for that kind of work

  • Normal recipes only start once that total reaches the recipe's requirement.
  • Star recipes and autopsy tasks bypass the normal threshold and begin at mastery 1 or above.
  • A zombie holds one tool or weapon and one armor, so pick the tool for its main job.
  • Patch 1.005 made pending zombie crafts show when the skill level is too low. If a queued craft shows that warning, it is a mastery problem, not a broken bench.

Tools: the fastest mastery boost

Give every new zombie the best tool you own for its job. It works the moment you equip it.

Smithing Rusty Hammer Bronze Hammer Iron Hammer Steel Hammer
Farming Rusty Shovel, Basic Rod Bronze Shovel, Amateur Rod Iron Shovel, Advanced Rod Steel Shovel, Pro Rod

Anatomy Butcher's Kit, Starter Alchemist's Kit Surgeon's Kit, Alchemist's Kit Master Surgeon's Kit, Good Alchemist's kit —

Watch the "tools" sets. Bronze tools give only +1 Smithing, Iron tools +2 and Steel tools +4. A plain Iron Hammer (+4) beats Iron tools (+2). Read the tooltip before you equip.

A shovel covers kitchen work, because cooking is Farming. Stations that need a specific tool are listed on the Zombies hub.

How zombies earn perk points

Zombies pay for their nodes with their own tech points, never the Keeper's:

  • Crafting. A zombie earns tech points each time it finishes work on a station.
  • Winning battles. Zombie fighters earn red tech points for won fights. Patches 1.006 and 1.007 both list this change. See how to get tech points for the Keeper's side.
  • Watching (one creator's trick). A zombie placed in front of a machine the Keeper started gained about 3 points when the craft finished, even without the mastery to run it. It's slow, but it gets a new zombie its first node.

To spend points, Inspect the zombie and open its perk trees. The first node on every tree is a +1 mastery upgrade (50 points on Building, Smithing and Farming; 25 blue on Book writing and Anatomy), and almost every perk sits behind it. The one exception is the Musician line on Book writing.

Perk slots: one per red skull

Every node a zombie buys takes one red skull, mastery upgrade or perk.

  • A zombie can purchase another node only when its red-skull count is higher than its current node count.
  • If its red count later falls below its node count, some nodes stop working until it has enough again.
  • White skulls give no slots.

So a 5-red zombie owns five nodes in total, across all five trees. Specialise: one zombie, one job. To add slots, put the zombie back on the autopsy table for red parts or swap in a higher collar. The steps are in build a 5 or 10 red skull worker. Golden collars need Smithing mastery 8 to craft; a soldier's stew gives +2 for a while.

Three example builds for a 5-red zombie

Five slots, five nodes. Start each tree with its 50-point +1 node.

Iron smith (550 red points)

  • Smithing +1, Novice Blacksmith, Smithing +1, Smithing +1, Advanced Blacksmith.
  • Result: +3 from nodes; with an Iron Hammer that's 7, or 11 when working iron.

Woodworker (650 red points)

  • Building +1, +1, +1, Apprentice Carpenter, Building +1.
  • Result: +4 from nodes, +2 with wood; with a Steel Axe that's 12 on wood recipes.

Gardener (550 green points)

  • Farming +1, +1, Dirt Digger, +1, +1.
  • Result: +4 from nodes, +2 when planting; with a Bronze Shovel that's 8 when planting.

A choir zombie only needs 3 slots for the Musician line, which pays 85 blue points in total. Singing isn't crafting, so let it earn its points on a crafting station first.

Suppliers and haulers: don't waste red skulls

A workstation zombie doesn't fetch its own inputs. A zombie on a Zombie Supplier Station brings materials to the workstation zombies in its area and clears finished goods into chests. It needs no tool and no perks, so give that job to a 0–1 red body. Porters and the factory follow the same idea; see supplier & factory roles.

One creator's early setup is one specialist per mastery you need, plus a carrier. That keeps you under your zombie limit, which cuts sermon rewards when you go over.

FAQ

Q: How should I spend a zombie's first perk points?
A: Buy the first +1 mastery node for the tree that matches its job, then add a job perk when you have a free red-skull slot. Nodes use the zombie's own tech points.

Q: How do I increase zombie mastery?
A: Three ways add together: the tool in its hand (+1 to +6), mastery nodes it buys on its own trees (+9 in total per tree), and job perks such as Master Carpenter (+4 when working with wood).

Q: How do zombies get perk points?
A: They earn their own tech points while crafting, and red tech points for winning battles. They never spend the Keeper's points. Open the zombie with Inspect to spend them.

Q: How many perks can a zombie have?
A: One node per red skull on its body. Mastery upgrades and perks both take a slot. A 5-red zombie can own 5 nodes; if its red count later drops, some nodes stop working until it has enough again.

Q: Which zombie perks are useless?
A: Wikily lists Novice Fisherman, Veteran Fisherman and Dead jeweler as having no effect, because zombies do no fishing and the jeweler recipes come out the same. Skip them.

Q: Do supplier zombies need mastery or perks?
A: No. A zombie on a Zombie Supplier Station needs no tool and no perks, so a 0–1 red body is fine for that job. Save high-red bodies for crafting stations.
