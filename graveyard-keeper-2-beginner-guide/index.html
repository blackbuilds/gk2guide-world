Graveyard Keeper 2 Beginner Guide: Day-1 Priorities (Not a Wiki Dump)

Checked for Graveyard Keeper 2 · patch 1.005 — numbers may change; re-check after updates.

This page is for a new Graveyard Keeper 2 player who wants a priority order and clear “done when” checkboxes — not another quest encyclopedia. Use the phase boards below, then deep-link into systems pages when you hit a named blocker.

Brand note: GK2 Guide owns decision trees and checklists. Item/recipe/quest DB depth belongs on Wikily (see Reference).

TL;DR — First-week priority order

Do first (rough order — confirm quest labels in-game):

  1. Stabilize energy / sleep / early food so you can keep working stations. → Energy, Insanity & Early Bread
  2. Learn corpse triage before you bury everything blindly. → Autopsy & Skulls
  3. Push the Dream Dust / Plague Doctor gate so zombies can unlock. → Dream Dust → Zombies
  4. Keep Faith / sermons and tech priority moving together (soft currency loop). → Faith & Sermons · Tech Tree Priority

Ignore for now (Week 1):

  • Full alchemy formula grinding / rune encyclopedia → later Alchemy & Runes
  • Factory / supplier / porter layout perfection → tease only → Automation
  • Red-skull min-max for “perfect” workers → Red Skull Zombies after basic labor exists
  • Copying Graveyard Keeper 1 Fandom organ tables (wrong game)

Four systems you must unlock early

Autopsy & skulls

Corpse handling is not optional flavor — it decides graveyard quality and which bodies become workers. Learn bury vs resurrect vs cremate triage first; organ encyclopedia later. → Graveyard Keeper 2 Autopsy & Skulls

Zombies

Workers are the sequel’s throughput engine. Treat Dream Dust / Plague Doctor as a gate, then move through unlock → assign → optimize on one hub. → Dream Dust · Zombies

Faith & sermons

Faith is a weekly soft currency, not a one-time tutorial popup. Couple it with tech spending so you don’t stall mid-week. → Faith & Sermons · Tech Tree Priority

Tech tree (priority, not catalog)

Wikily owns the full tree. You need an opinionated early order that unblocks Science, zombies, church, and production. → Tech Tree Priority

FAQ

Q: What should I do in the first hour vs save for later?
A: Hour 1 is orientation + energy hygiene + locating morgue/church/tech UI. Save alchemy DB grinding, factory layout, and red-skull min-max. Use the Hour 1 table above as the checkbox list.

Q: What soft-locks progress in week 1?
A: High-level gates: Dream Dust / Plague Doctor (zombies), Faith/sermons (currency), and tech points spent on the wrong nodes. See Dream Dust and Faith.

Q: When do I bury vs prepare corpses for zombies?
A: Conceptual rule: bury bodies that keep graveyard quality healthy; hold/prepare candidates once resurrection is online; cremate bodies that only hurt the yard. Full triage flowchart → Autopsy & Skulls. Do not import GK1 organ tables.

Q: When do zombies become available, and why care about bread early?
A: Zombies unlock after the Dream Dust / Plague Doctor gate, then Resurrection Lab / Reanimation for ongoing workers. Bread (and sleep) keep energy up so you can finish that gate and still autopsy — → Energy/Food · Zombies.

Q: What is “done” for Day 1 / Day 3 / Week 1?
A: Day 1: energy loop + corpse path known. Day 3: bread ladder + autopsy habit + Dream Dust path progressing + one sermon cycle. Week 1: first workers assigned + Faith/tech loops routine. Use the three phase boards as the definition of done.

Q: What should I not grind yet?
A: Full alchemy formulas, full cooking encyclopedia, factory perfection, and late red-skull optimization. Those belong later (or on Wikily as DB). Automation checklist comes after basic workers → Automation.

Q: Is this a walkthrough of every quest?
A: No. This is priority order. For quest DB detail, use Wikily outbound (Reference). For early narrative route density, Fextralife’s walkthrough exists — our wedge is phase boards + blockers.

Q: Can I use Graveyard Keeper 1 tips?
A: Not for organs, skull math, or tech order. GK1 Fandom ranks on some SERPs — treat it as wrong franchise unless you re-verify the same rule in GK2.
