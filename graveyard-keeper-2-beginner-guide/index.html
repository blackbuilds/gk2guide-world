Graveyard Keeper 2 Beginner Guide: Day-1 Checklist That Unblocks Play

This page is for a new Graveyard Keeper 2 player who wants a priority order and clear “done when” checkboxes — not another quest encyclopedia. Use the phase boards below, then jump to the systems guides when you hit a named blocker.

For item, recipe, and quest details, see Wikily (Reference below).

TL;DR — First-week priority order

Day 1 in Graveyard Keeper 2: clear autopsy triage → get a worker zombie online → unlock the tech that feeds your first board — use the checklists below.

Do first (rough order — confirm quest labels in-game):

  1. Stabilize energy / sleep / early food so you can keep working stations. → Energy, Insanity & Early Bread
  2. Learn corpse triage before you bury everything blindly. → Autopsy & Skulls
  3. Push the Dream Dust / Plague Doctor gate so zombies can unlock. → Dream Dust → Zombies
  4. Keep Faith / sermons and tech priority moving together (soft currency loop). → Faith & Sermons · Tech Tree Priority

Hour-1 checklist (first 60 minutes)

Quick checklist — tick these before you tourist the tech tree or wander into distant clearings. Tip-video labels that disagree — confirm in UI.

  • [ ] Pin starter food + clear the base — pin cheese/bread (or whatever is in home chests) to the hotbar; spend opening energy smashing boxes and clearing trees/rocks around the yard (buildable area) before distant non-buildable tourist clearings.
  • [ ] Stash zone chests; learn zone-local inventory — each map zone shares its own chest inventory (yard ≠ garden ≠ house). Leave workshop stock in that zone’s chests; don’t expect yard chests to feed the garden. Confirm exact zone labels in your build.
  • [ ] Follow first quests / burial path to tools — walk the quest signs through the first burial / certificate loop; early tools (shovel/hammer from story chests after burial) raise mastery 0→1 and unlock real crafts. → Autopsy & Skulls
  • [ ] Unlock gardening or wood cluster only as quests demand — bank tech points; open the node the active quest needs (beds / millstone / chopping / woodworking). Do not tourist-unlock late cosmetics, deep alchemy, or factory nodes Day 1. → Tech Tree Priority
  • [ ] Sleep when tired (save + insanity) — sleep on the Lack of Sleep icon; sleep also saves. Sleeping while not tired does not clear insanity the same way. → Energy, Insanity & Early Bread

Ignore for now (Week 1):

  • Full alchemy formula grinding / rune encyclopedia → later Alchemy & Runes
  • Factory / supplier / porter layout perfection → preview only → Automation
  • Red-skull min-max for “perfect” workers → Red Skull Zombies after basic labor exists
  • Copying Graveyard Keeper 1 Fandom organ tables (wrong game)

Phase board: Week 1

Week-1 unlock order: keep tech tree priority aligned with Faith and your first worker board — don’t spend tech points on late factory, combat mats, or vanity nodes until a quest or board demands them. Tip videos sometimes push a wood → food → stone unlock sequence or a graveyard-quality target — treat those as Must-tier cluster hints, not settled fact; confirm node labels and thresholds in your tech UI after patches.

Four systems you must unlock early

Autopsy & skulls

Corpse handling is not optional flavor — it decides graveyard quality and which bodies become workers. Learn bury vs resurrect vs cremate triage first; organ encyclopedia later. → Graveyard Keeper 2 Autopsy & Skulls

Zombies

Workers are the sequel’s throughput engine. Treat Dream Dust / Plague Doctor as a gate, then move through unlock → assign → optimize on one hub. Early zombies need a Zombie Supply Station + correct tools/mastery or the craft queue sits idle. → Dream Dust · Zombies

Faith & sermons

Faith is a weekly soft currency, not a one-time tutorial popup. Couple it with tech spending so you don’t stall mid-week. → Faith & Sermons · Tech Tree Priority

Tech tree (priority, not catalog)

Wikily has the full tree. You need an opinionated early order that unblocks Science, zombies, church, and production — unlock when needed, not when curious. → Tech Tree Priority

FAQ

Q: What should I do in the first hour vs save for later?
A: Hour 1 is pin food + clear the yard, stash zone chests, follow the first burial/quest path to tools, unlock only quest-demanded tech, and sleep when tired. Save deep alchemy grinding, factory layout, and red-skull min-max. Use the Hour-1 checklist under TL;DR (five boxes) plus the Hour 1 phase table as your checkbox list.

Q: What soft-locks progress in week 1?
A: High-level gates: Dream Dust / Plague Doctor (zombies), Faith/sermons (currency), and tech points spent on the wrong nodes. See Dream Dust and Faith.

Q: When do I bury vs prepare corpses for zombies?
A: Conceptual rule: white skulls → graves (yard quality); red skulls → worker candidates (perk slots); else cremate. Once Plague Doctor / Dream Dust–reanimation is up, stop mass-burying middling corpses to clear the morgue — triage instead. Full flowchart → Autopsy & Skulls. Do not import GK1 organ tables.

Q: When do zombies become available, and why care about a cooked food loop early?
A: Zombies unlock after the Dream Dust / Plague Doctor gate, then Resurrection Lab / Reanimation for ongoing workers. Pick one renewable cooked loop (wheat→bread or pies or baked forage) and sleep so you can finish that gate and still autopsy — tip videos disagree on which staple is “best”; don’t chase every recipe. Tip-list energy numbers — confirm in your cook UI. → Energy/Food · Zombies.

Q: How should I spend early Faith?
A: Faith comes from the sermon day (commonly Wrath / red mark — confirm calendar icon in UI). Spend early Faith on map movement unlocks and key Inspiration claims before emptying into vanity church décor. Tip videos sometimes claim church quality only feeds town gratitude and cite a low quality figure as “enough” — treat tip-list figures as hints only; confirm church quality in UI, not a hard build target. → Faith & Sermons.

Q: What is “done” for Day 1 / Day 3 / Week 1?
A: Day 1: energy loop + zone chests + corpse path known. Day 3: one cooked staple loop + autopsy habit + Dream Dust path progressing + one sermon cycle. Week 1: first workers assigned + Faith/tech loops routine + stop mass-burying. Use the three phase boards as the definition of done.

Q: What should I not grind yet?
A: Full alchemy formulas, full cooking encyclopedia, factory perfection, and late red-skull optimization. Those belong later (or on Wikily). Automation checklist comes after basic workers → Automation.

Q: Demo carryover / zombie caps — still true?
A: Some tip videos are demo-era (carryover of demo progress, demo zombie caps, demo end gates). Prefer full-release UI and Steam patch notes over demo numbers — ignore or re-check any tip that still says Demo. → Steam announcements app 4358690.

Q: Is this a walkthrough of every quest?
A: No. This is priority order. For quest details, use Wikily (see Reference). For a full story walkthrough, see Fextralife’s walkthrough — this guide focuses on phase boards + blockers.

Q: Can I use Graveyard Keeper 1 tips?
A: Not for organs, skull math, or tech order. GK1 Fandom pages still show up in search results — treat them as wrong franchise unless you re-check the same rule in GK2.
